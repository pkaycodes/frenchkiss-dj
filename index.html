02 About

Behind the decks.

The booth · Accra

FrenchKiss DJ is a DJ based in Accra, Ghana — a regular behind the decks at the city's club nights, brand events and private parties, and the selector behind FKTV Live.

His sets run on amapiano log drums, Afrobeats and afro-house, with dancehall and hip-hop thrown in when the room asks for it. Long blends, clean transitions, and refixes he cuts himself the week before the gig.

FKTV Live is where those edits land: mashups, flips and full recorded sets, filmed in the booth and posted to YouTube and SoundCloud. It is the closest thing to standing next to the decks without paying cover.

Nana Kojo Adjei by name, the selection has carried him from Accra club nights to Tech in Ghana’s Kumasi Royal Edition, and to rooms in London. Same brief wherever he plays: read the floor, then take it somewhere.
